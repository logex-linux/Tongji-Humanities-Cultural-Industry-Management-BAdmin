摘要：在全球产业链供应链加速重构的背景下，制度型开放通过打破要素流动壁垒、重塑产业国际竞争优势、拓宽企业国际化经营空间，为产业链韧性提升提供重要支撑。使用 — 年中国地级及以上城市面板

20102024数据，以自由贸易试验区（以下简称“自贸试验区”）设立作为准自然实验，研究制度型开放对产业链韧性的影响。从产业链抵抗力、恢复力、再组织力以及更新力四个维度构建产业链韧性指标评价体系测度各城市产业链韧性水平，并采用双重差分法进行实证研究发现，自贸试验区能够显著提升产业链韧性。机制检验发现，自贸试验区通过优化要素配置、推动城市全球价值链攀升以及助力企业海外布局提升产业链韧性。调节作用检验发现，自贸试验区的央地协同和地地协同能够正向调节制度型开放对产业链韧性的提升作用。异质性分析表明，自贸试验区对产业链韧性的提升作用在东部地区、大规模城市中更为强烈。研究结论为我国深入推进制度型开放以及维护产业链供应链安全稳定提供了政策启示。

Does Institutional Opening up Enhance Industrial Chain Resilience

Evidence from A Quasi Natural Experiment Based

on China s Pilot Free Trade Zones

Ning Jingbo，Zhao Xiaoguang


**Abstract**

：Against the backdrop of accelerated restructuring of global industrial and supply chains，institutional opening-up provides critical support for enhancing industrial chain resilience by breaking down barriers to factor mobility，reshaping international competitive advantages of industries，and expanding firms’ space for international operations. Using panel data from 2010 to 2024 covering all prefecture-level and above cities in China，this study leverages Pilot Free Trade Zones （PFTZs）as a quasi-natural experiment to investigate the impact of institutional opening-up on industrial chain resilience. An evaluation system of industrial chain resilience is constructed across four dimensions—resistance，recovery，reorganization，and renewal—to measure the resilience level of each city.Employing a difference-in-differences （DID）methodology，empirical analysis demonstrates that PFTZs significantly enhance industrial chain resilience. Mechanism tests reveal that PFTZs improve resilience through optimizing factor allocation，facilitating cities’ upgrading in the global value chain，and supporting firms’ overseas expansion. Moderating effect analysis indicates that both central-local collaborative governance and inter-regional collaborative governance positively strengthen the effect of institutional opening-up on industrial chain resilience. Heterogeneity analysis further shows that the positive impact of PFTZs is more pronounced in eastern regions and in large-scale cities. These findings provide policy implications for China’s continued advancement of institutional opening-up and the maintenance of secure and stable industrial and supply chains.Key words

：institutional opening-up；industrial chain resilience；Pilot Free Trade Zones （PFTZs）
