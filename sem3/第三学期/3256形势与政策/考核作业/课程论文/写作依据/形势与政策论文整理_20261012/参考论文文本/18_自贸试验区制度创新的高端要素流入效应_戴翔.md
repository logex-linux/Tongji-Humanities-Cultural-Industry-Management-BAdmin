摘要: 形成和集聚高端要素是夯实经济高质量发展基石的关键。本文在创新性地测度制度创新水平的基础上, 基于双向固定效应模型, 采用个自贸片区及其对应的个地级市数据, 实证检验了自贸试验区制度创新对高端要素流入的影响。检验结果表明: 自贸试验区制度创新主要通过知识产权保护、贸易便利化和产业优化升级等机制, 促进高端要素流入, 其中教育水平在此过程中起调节作用。实证研究还发现, 自贸试验区制度创新促进高端要素流入的现实效应, 因城市区位、设立批次、城市规模而有所差异。本文的研究结论为进一步推进自贸试验区制度创新,实现高端要素流入, 从而夯实经济高质量发展提供经验证据。


**关键词: 自贸试验区; 高端要素; 制度创新**

The Impact of Institutional Innovation on the Inflow of High-end

Factors in Pilot Free Trade Zones

DAI Xiang ZOU Xiaoyi

(Joint Research Institute, Nanjing Audit University, Nanjing, Jiangsu,211815)


**Abstract**

: The formation and aggregation of high-end factors are crucial for consolida- ting the cornerstone of high-quality economic development. This article, based on an inno- vative measurement of institutional innovation levels, employed a two-way fixed-effects model using data from 29 pilot free trade zones and their corresponding 17 prefecture-level cities to empirically examine the impact of institutional innovation in free trade zones on the inflow of high-end factors. The empirical results indicate that institutional innovation in free trade zones primarily promotes the inflow of high-end factors through mechanisms such as intellectual property protection, trade facilitation, and industrial upgrading, with edu- cational levels playing a moderating role. The empirical research also reveals that the real effects of institutional innovation in free trade zones on promoting the inflow of high-end factors vary depending on factors such as the location of urban, the sequence of establish- ment, and the size of city. The research conclusions of this article provide empirical evi- dence for further advancing institutional innovation in free trade zones, thereby facilitating the inflow of high-end factors and ultimately solidifying the foundation for high-quality eco- nomic development.
